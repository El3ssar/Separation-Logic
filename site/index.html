About these solutions

Every Lean proof in this workbook is machine-checked. The full corpus of milestones M0–M13 — 127 theorems — compiles as a single self-contained file under Lean 4.32.2 with no imports, no Mathlib, and zero sorrys. Where a statement in the original syllabus was unprovable as written, the corrected statement is given and the reason is explained in the surrounding text.
